# Attaching Photos

Photos help connect experimental evidence to the exact step where it was observed.

## Examples

- SDS-PAGE gel image.
- SEC chromatogram screenshot.
- Column setup photo.
- Precipitation or aggregation photo.

## Current Behavior

1. Open a workflow or experiment step from the `Workflow` page.
2. In the side editor, go to `Photo Attachments`.
3. Click `Upload` and choose an image file.
4. The image appears immediately in the editor.
5. Click the delete control on a thumbnail if you want to remove an outdated image.
6. Save the step if you are also changing notes or status.

## Supported Files

- PNG
- JPEG
- WEBP
- GIF
- SVG

Local files are stored under `data/uploads` and attachment metadata is saved in SQLite.
